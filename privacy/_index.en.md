## 🍪 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

### What are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites remember your preferences and provide a better user experience.

### Cookies We Use

This site uses the following types of cookies:

#### 1. Necessary Cookies
- **Purpose**: Ensure the website functions properly
- **Examples**: Session management, security settings
- **Can be disabled**: No (required for website functionality)

#### 2. Analytics Cookies
- **Purpose**: Understand how visitors use the website
- **Service Provider**: Google Analytics (if enabled)
- **Data Collected**: Page views, visit duration, bounce rate, etc.
- **Can be disabled**: Yes

#### 3. Advertising Cookies
- **Purpose**: Display relevant advertisements
- **Service Provider**: Google AdSense
- **Data Collected**: Browsing behavior, interest preferences
- **Can be disabled**: Yes

### How to Manage Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies through the following methods:

- **Browser Settings**: Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies
- **Opt-out of Personalized Ads**: Visit [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads)
- **Browser Plugins**: Use ad blockers or privacy protection plugins

**Please Note**: Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality and user experience of the website.
