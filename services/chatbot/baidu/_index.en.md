## 🎯 Service Overview

ERNIE Bot (Wenxin Yiyan) is Baidu's generative AI conversation service. Starting from April 1, 2025, Baidu announced that ERNIE Bot will be fully free and open to all PC and App users.

**Core Advantages:**
- 🎉 **Fully Free from 4/1/2025** - Available on PC and mobile
- 🆕 **Latest Models** - ERNIE 4.5 multimodal, ERNIE X1 reasoning

## 🔧 FAQ

### 1. When will it be fully free?

**Answer:** From April 1, 2025, ERNIE Bot is fully free for all users.

### 2. Is real-name verification required?

**Answer:** Chatbot doesn't require real-name verification, only Baidu account. API service requires verification.

### 3. Are there usage limits?

**Answer:** Officially announced as fully free, no explicit limits currently, but may queue during peak hours.

### 4. What devices are supported?

**Answer:** 
- PC: Access via browser at https://yiyan.baidu.com
- Mobile: Download "Wenxin Yiyan" app (iOS/Android)
- Tablet: Supports tablet browser and app

### 5. What's the difference from API?

**Answer:**
- **Chatbot:** Web/App conversation, for general users, completely free
- **API:** Programming interface, for developers, has free and paid models

### 6. Can overseas users access?

**Answer:** Yes, but:
- Primarily serves mainland China users
- Overseas access may be slower
- App may not be available in some regional app stores
